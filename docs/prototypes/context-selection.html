Quarterly review · Q3 2026

Activation is growing. Confidence is not.

The new onboarding path gets more teams to their first published board, but the handoff from creation to collaboration remains unclear.

Product strategy6 min readUpdated today

Finding: the activation gap moved downstream

First-board creation improved from 41% to 58% after the template launch. The sharper issue now appears after publishing: only 23% of new workspaces invite a collaborator in their first week.

Interviews suggest the page feels complete once it is published. The collaboration action reads like administration, not the next meaningful step.

First board58%+17 points
First invite23%+2 points
Week-2 return31%+4 points

Recommendation: make sharing consequential

Place collaboration inside the completion moment. After a board is published, ask who needs to react to it and preview the message they will receive.

Success means improving first-week collaborator invites without reducing publishing completion or adding a mandatory setup step.
